Standing bucket for defects filed from the tools themselves: sbt's legacy `:bug` route and repository-scoped `b` capture assign defects here (the latter by name-keyed membership, even without this definition). Unlike a shipped-and-done project this one never completes; it is a triage queue, and its roll-up percentage reads as "how much of the known defect load is cleared", not "how close to release". Legacy tool reports default to medium priority; repository captures default to low priority, Not started, Considering (unplanned), no assignee and one confirmation AC. Groom either kind before dispatching it when those defaults need replacing.
